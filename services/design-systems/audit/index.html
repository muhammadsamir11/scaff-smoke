The two weeks

  1. 01

    Days 1–3: Collect

    Every color, size and space value in production, counted by where it appears.

  2. 02

    Days 4–7: Group

    Values within a step of each other are grouped and given one name.

  3. 03

    Days 8–10: Hand over

    A change list your team can apply: which value becomes which token, file by file.
