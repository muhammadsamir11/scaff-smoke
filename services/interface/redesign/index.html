The weeks

  1. 01

    Week 1: Pick the screens

    We agree on the three to six screens and what each must make clear first.

  2. 02

    Weeks 2–3: Redraw

    New layouts for each screen, built from your components, reviewed live.

  3. 03

    Weeks 4–6: Ship

    The screens land in your code behind a flag, then go out to everyone.
