Legal · Privacy

Privacy policy.

What we collect when you contact the studio, how we use it, and the choices you have. Effective March 2026.

The policy

  1. 01

    What we collect

    When you use the project brief, we receive the fields you submit: name, work email, links you share, and whatever you write in the form. If you email us directly, we keep that correspondence. We do not sell lists or buy contact data.

  2. 02

    How we use it

    We use your message to reply, scope work, and run an engagement if we proceed together. We may store it in email and in tools we use to run the studio (for example, a shared inbox or a lightweight CRM). We do not use it for unrelated marketing.

  3. 03

    How long we keep it

    We keep inquiry and project records while a conversation or contract is active, and for a reasonable period afterward so we can refer to prior work. You can ask us to delete what we hold about you unless we need to keep a copy for legal or accounting reasons.

  4. 04

    Cookies and analytics

    This site is mostly static pages. If we add analytics later, we will describe what runs and why here. Today we do not run third-party ad trackers on scaff.studio pages.

  5. 05

    Contact

    Questions about this policy or your data: write through Start a project or email privacy@scaff.studio. We will answer within a reasonable time.

Changes

We may update this page when our tools or obligations change. The date at the top of the hero will move when we do. Material changes will be summarized here in plain language.
