Data · Color

More on screen, still readable.

An agronomy dashboard. Density went up, and the contrast on chart labels went up with it.

See the results All work
Client
Agronomy platform
Length

Team

The problem

Agronomists used Plot in the field, on tablets in bright light. The charts used six greens that were hard to tell apart outdoors.

We moved to four colors chosen for contrast in sunlight, put labels on the lines instead of in a legend, and fit two more fields on every screen.
